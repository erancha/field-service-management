⚡ Field Service Management

A walkthrough of one real run

Field Service Management books maintenance visits for agencies — elevators, appliances, anything with a technician and a van. What follows is one uninterrupted session with it running: an empty database at the start, a booked appointment sitting in two Google Calendars at the end, and every screen in between in the order it happened.

Nothing here is a mockup. Every screen is the running stack, which is why the browser chrome and the localhost ports are left in view. Three roles — back office, technician and customer — run as three separate apps on three ports, and three different people sign in over the course of the run.

About these screenshots

Captured from a single local run on 2 Aug 2026, in chronological order, unedited apart from privacy redaction. The localhost addresses and browser chrome are deliberately left visible as evidence that this is the running stack rather than a design mockup.

Pixelated regions are unrelated Google accounts that happened to be signed in on the machine, the OAuth client id, and a consent session token. Customer names, addresses and phone numbers shown are demo data.

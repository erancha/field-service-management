Field Service Management — a walkthrough of one real run

Twenty-five screenshots from a single uninterrupted session, in the order they were taken. It starts with an empty database and ends with a booked appointment sitting in two Google Calendars. Nothing here is a mockup — every screen is the running stack, which is why the browser chrome and the localhost ports are left in view.

Three roles run as three separate apps, each on its own port, driven from three browser profiles signed in as three different Google accounts.

About these screenshots

Captured from a single local run on 2 Aug 2026, in chronological order, unedited apart from privacy redaction. The localhost addresses and browser chrome are deliberately left visible as evidence that this is the running stack rather than a design mockup.

Pixelated regions are unrelated Google accounts that happened to be signed in on the machine, the OAuth client id, and a consent session token. Customer names, addresses and phone numbers shown are demo data.
